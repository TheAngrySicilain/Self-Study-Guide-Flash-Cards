Month 1 Study Guide Flash Cards

Print at 100% / Actual Size. Do not select “Fit to Page.” Cut along the solid borders, then glue each card onto a 3×5 index card.

Each landscape page places a question/front on the left and its matching answer/back on the right. Each card is exactly 5×3 inches for a landscape index card. Cut around the solid borders, then fold or glue the matching halves together. Print one test page first.
